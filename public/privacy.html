HB+ Team Space

Privacy

HB+ Team Space is a demo app built for the HB+ hiring assignment. It lets people sign in with Google and only admits @hbplus.fit accounts.

What it stores

When you sign in, Google shares your name, email address and profile photo. If your email is on an allowed domain, the app keeps those in its database together with your role (member or admin) and a log of your sign-ins. If your email isn't allowed, no account is created.

What it's used for

Only to decide whether you can get in and to show you the dashboard. Nothing is sold or shared with anyone, and there are no ads or tracking scripts.

Where it lives

The data is stored in Supabase. Your session is kept in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in, and it's cleared when you sign out.

Deleting your data

Open an issue on the project's GitHub page and your account and sign-in history will be deleted.
